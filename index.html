Edit node

On a tablet: drag with one finger to pan, pinch with two to zoom. Double-tap the background to fit everything.

To move a node: press and hold it for a moment, then drag — a green outline shows where it will land. Drop on a node to nest it inside; drop beside one to slot it in next to it.

For long moves across a big tree, use ✋ Pick up on the node, scroll to the destination, tap it, then ⤵ Drop into. Indent / Outdent shift a node one level without dragging.

Several parents: a node can belong to as many parents as you like, and they are all equal. Tap the node, press 🔗 Link, then tap every node it should belong to. Tap one again to remove it. Press ✓ Done (or tap empty space) to finish. The node sits centred underneath all its parents.
✂ Unlink opens a list of its parents: tick the ones to disconnect, or tick All.
The + / − button on any of its parents shows or hides it for all of them. Deleting one of its parents keeps the node — it just stays under the others.

Topics are the tabs at the top — tap + New topic to start a fresh tree for another reading.

Panel size: drag the panel's edge to make it bigger or smaller. Double-click the handle to reset it.

Sign in with Google once on each device and everything syncs automatically — phone, tablet and computer all show the latest version. The dot next to the title shows the sync state. Your notes are stored privately in your own Firebase database, never in this page.
