On a tablet: drag with one finger to pan, pinch with two to zoom. Double-tap the background to fit everything.

To move a node: press and hold it for a moment, then drag — a green outline shows where it will land. Drop on a node to nest it inside; drop beside one to slot it in next to it.

For long moves across a big tree, use ✋ Pick up on the node, scroll to the destination, tap it, then ⤵ Drop into. Indent / Outdent shift a node one level without dragging.

Topics are the tabs at the top — tap + New topic to start a fresh tree for another reading.
